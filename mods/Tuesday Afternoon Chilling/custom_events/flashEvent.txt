The flash flashes only the color white

value 1:
The alpha value of the flash, for example:
0 = a flash will not even happen
0.5 = a flash will happen but slightly visible
1 = a full white color flash

value 2: the duration it will take for the flash to fully disappear
(either 1 or 0.8 are recommende, you can make it longer if youd like)
